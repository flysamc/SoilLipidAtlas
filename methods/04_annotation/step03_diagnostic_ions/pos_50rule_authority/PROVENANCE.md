## What Table S3 is

The 50-rule executable classifier is the source of truth for Table S3:
24 diagnostic fragment rules, five neutral-loss rules, and 21
terpenoid-fragment rules across 13 classifier labels. A separate, older
33-entry quick-reference table is literature-oriented background and is
not substituted for the classifier.
