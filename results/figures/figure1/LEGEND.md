# Figure 1 — legend of record

**Figure 1 | Distributed lipid fingerprints resolve soil community composition
across six organism groups.** **a**, Reference lipidome atlas. Cultured soil
organisms spanning six ecological display groups (Bacteria, Archaea, Fungi,
Viridiplantae, Animalia and Protists) and 19 collected phyla (16 of which meet
the two-mode replication threshold for analysis) were profiled by untargeted
LC-MS/MS in six acquisition batches. Per-batch processing and cross-batch
alignment yield one consensus feature atlas per ionisation mode (273,248
positive-mode and 122,571 negative-mode features). **b**, Two complementary
analytical layers. First, phylum-enriched biomarkers are selected by the union
of composite scoring and indicator value (IndVal) analysis (11,371 biomarkers
across 16 phyla in positive mode), assigned an annotation confidence tier
(Gold, molecular species; Silver, partial structure; Bronze, lipid class;
Unidentified) and validated by detection in public soil MS/MS data (fastMASST).
Second, Bray–Curtis dissimilarity over all quality-filtered features recovers
taxonomic structure, and the features that best discriminate each phylum define
its distributed fingerprint (SIMPER), with confirmation from independent
selection methods (SCBD, CAP, L1) and MS2LDA substructure motifs. **c**, Soil
community decoding. Grassland soils from the ClimGrass climate manipulation
experiment (2 × 2 factorial, ambient or future climate crossed with drought,
n = 3 per treatment) are profiled identically; MS/MS spectral matching against
the atlas defines a spectrally verified substrate of 736 diagnostic fingerprint
features spanning the 16 phyla, and each soil lipidome is decomposed into
phylum contributions against the reference fingerprints. **d**, From lipid
signal to community composition. Matched signals are made comparable across
lipids and phyla by quantification correction (response-factor calibration,
splitting of phylum-shared biomarkers, and an archaeal estimate restricted to
validated ether lipids), validated by a leave-one-out negative control on pure
isolates (Supplementary Fig. 5), yielding a phylum-resolved community
composition estimate. The composition plot shown is schematic; quantitative
estimates and confidence intervals are reported in Fig. 5. Abbreviations:
LC-MS/MS, liquid chromatography–tandem mass spectrometry; IndVal, indicator
value; SIMPER, similarity percentage analysis; SCBD, species contribution to
beta diversity; CAP, canonical analysis of principal coordinates; L1,
L1-regularised selection stability; MS2LDA, MS/MS substructure discovery by
latent Dirichlet allocation; fastMASST, fast mass spectrometry search tool.
