## The four that need a decision, not just a rerun

**M3-S5 — "104 features flagged as RT-uncertain" (UNVERIFIABLE).** No located producer
yields 104. The method as described, IQR within molecular families, cannot approach it:
97% of families hold fewer than four members, so no IQR can be computed for them. The
documented method gives 11 (POS) and 23 (NEG); grouping by lipid class gives 97 (POS),
close to 104, and the only recovered RT producer does group by class. Either recover the
producer or correct the Methods sentence.

**M3-S8-b — Step 8 phylum impact figures (DEAD_LABEL).** The sentence reads
"Euryarchaeota 15 to 638 … Crenarchaeota 188 to 289". Under `taxonomy_policy.json`,
Euryarchaeota and Halobacteriota map to Methanobacteriota and Crenarchaeota to
Thermoproteota, so those labels no longer exist as analysis phyla and the before/after
framing does not parse. The strict ArchLips assignment is 879 Methanobacteriota + 216
Thermoproteota. This sentence must be rewritten, not merely renumbered.

**M3-S8-c — negative-mode archaeal annotation (UNVERIFIABLE).** 0 Gold+Silver across 21
NEG archaeal features, and 0 diagnostic ions and 0 neutral losses across all 21 spectra,
which are otherwise good quality (12–160 peaks, median 49). The 10 Bronze calls rest on
mass accuracy and detection rate alone. Negative-mode archaeal annotation cannot be
presented as validated.

**M3-S6 — POS RT sum-composition prediction (UNVERIFIABLE).** The Mac recovery archive
reconciles the frozen counts: 980 primary plus 784 disjoint hybrid predictions = 1,764,
and 403 hybrid predictions have more than 60 carbons. However, the later historical
final table removes all 784 hybrid predictions, not only those 403. The exact POS caller,
five-feature contract and application/downgrade code remain absent. These are historical
output counts, not a strict rerun. The user approved replacement POS v1, which stages
one medium-confidence candidate review-only and performs zero tier changes; it must not
be described as reproduction of the submitted Step 6.
