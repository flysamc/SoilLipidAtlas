# Figure 1 — legend of record

**Figure 1 | Distributed lipid fingerprints resolve soil community composition
across six organism groups.** **a**, Reference lipidomes: cultured soil
organisms spanning six organism groups (Bacteria, Archaea, Fungi,
Viridiplantae, Animalia, Protists) and 19 collected phyla (16 phyla with
sufficient replication in both ionisation modes enter all statistics) were
profiled by untargeted LC-MS/MS in six acquisition batches; per-batch
processing and cross-batch alignment yield one consensus feature atlas per
ionisation mode (273,248 positive-mode and 122,571 negative-mode aligned
features). **b**, Two analytical layers: phylum-enriched biomarkers, selected
by the union of composite scoring and Indicator Value analysis (11,371
biomarkers across 16 phyla in positive mode), each assigned an annotation
confidence tier (Gold, molecular species-level assignment; Silver, partial
assignment; Bronze, lipid-class assignment; Unidentified, no confident
structural assignment) and validated by detection in public soil MS/MS data
(fastMASST); and distributed fingerprints, in which Bray-Curtis dissimilarity
over all quality-filtered features recovers taxonomic structure and each
phylum receives the set of features that most distinguish it (SIMPER),
confirmed by independent fingerprint-construction methods (SCBD, CAP, L1) and
MS2LDA substructure motifs. **c**, Soil community decoded: grassland soils
from the ClimGrass climate manipulation experiment (2 × 2 factorial, ambient
versus future climate crossed with drought, n = 3 per treatment) were
profiled identically; MS/MS spectral matching against the atlas defines a
spectrally verified substrate of 736 diagnostic fingerprint features spanning
the 16 phyla, and each soil lipidome is decomposed into phylum contributions
against the reference fingerprints. **d**, From lipid signal to community
composition: matched signal is made comparable across lipids and phyla by
quantification correction (response-factor calibration; weight-splitting of
biomarkers shared between phyla; an archaeal estimate restricted to validated
ether lipids), the framework is validated by a leave-one-out negative control
on pure isolates (Supplementary Fig. 5), and the output is a phylum-resolved
community composition estimate; the composition plot shown is schematic, with
quantitative estimates and confidence intervals reported in Fig. 5.
Abbreviations: LC-MS/MS, liquid chromatography-tandem mass spectrometry;
SIMPER, similarity percentage analysis; SCBD, species contribution to beta
diversity; CAP, canonical analysis of principal coordinates; L1,
L1-regularised selection stability; MS2LDA, MS/MS substructure discovery by
latent Dirichlet allocation; fastMASST, fast mass spectrometry search tool.
