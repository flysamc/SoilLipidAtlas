## The four claims that required a decision, not just a rerun

**M3-S5 — "104 features flagged as RT-uncertain" (❓ UNVERIFIABLE).** No
located producer yields 104. The method as described — IQR within molecular
families — cannot approach it: 97 % of families hold fewer than four members,
so no IQR can be computed for them. The documented method gives 11 (POS) and
23 (NEG); grouping by lipid class gives 97 (POS), close to 104, and the only
recovered RT producer does group by class. The revised Methods states the
grouping actually used (see `../step05_rt_validation/STEP5_REPORT.md`).

**M3-S8-b — Step 8 phylum impact figures (🏷 DEAD_LABEL).** The sentence reads
"Euryarchaeota 15 to 638 … Crenarchaeota 188 to 289". Under
`taxonomy_policy.json`, Euryarchaeota and Halobacteriota map to
Methanobacteriota and Crenarchaeota to Thermoproteota, so those labels no
longer exist as analysis phyla and the before/after framing does not parse.
The strict ArchLips assignment is 879 Methanobacteriota + 216 Thermoproteota;
the sentence is rewritten, not merely renumbered.

**M3-S8-c — negative-mode archaeal annotation (❓ UNVERIFIABLE).** 0
Gold+Silver across 21 NEG archaeal features, and 0 diagnostic ions and 0
neutral losses across all 21 spectra, which are otherwise good quality
(12–160 peaks, median 49). The 10 Bronze calls rest on mass accuracy and
detection rate alone. Negative-mode archaeal annotation is therefore never
presented as validated identification.

**M3-S6 — POS RT sum-composition prediction (❓ UNVERIFIABLE).** The recovery
archive reconciles the frozen counts: 980 primary plus 784 disjoint hybrid
predictions = 1,764, and 403 hybrid predictions have more than 60 carbons.
However, the later historical final table removes all 784 hybrid predictions,
not only those 403. The exact POS caller, five-feature contract and
application/downgrade code remain absent. The adopted replacement (POS v1)
stages one medium-confidence candidate review-only and performs zero tier
changes; it is a declared replacement, never described as a reproduction of
the submitted Step 6.

## The five pending at audit time

Step 7, Method 6 and the final integrated Results claim were incomplete when
this audit ran, so the submitted 72.3 % headline annotation rate could not be
restated then. Harmonisation values at audit time were a floor: POS 55.3 %
annotated / 15.0 % Gold+Silver, NEG 31.4 % / 9.0 %. (Method 6 — the public
fastMASST/Pan-ReDU search — has since completed; see
`methods/05_public_validation/`. Step 10's SIRIUS gap-fill has also since
completed; see the step-04 README.) Steps 6 and 9 moved from pending to
UNVERIFIABLE once recovery established that their historical outputs exist
but their exact action producers do not. Step 7's 124-entry custom archaeal
database is absent from every archive.
